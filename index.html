Oikos Health

Oikos Health is a personal, single-user app. It lets its owner read their own health record (labs, medications, allergies and conditions) through their health system's patient access API, so their personal assistant software can plan meals around it.

It runs only on the owner's own computer. It is read only, it never writes to any health record, and it is not offered to the public or to other patients.
